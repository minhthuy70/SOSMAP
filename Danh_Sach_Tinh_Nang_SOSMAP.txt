12. TOM TAT PHIEN BAN HIEN TAI

- He thong dang co giao dien SOS MAP voi thanh dieu huong co ban.
- Ban do Leaflet duoc hien thi tren nen OpenStreetMap.
- Ban do dat mac dinh tai khu vuc Thanh pho Ho Chi Minh.
- He thong dang co mot marker mau cho su co o ga hoac chuong ngai vat.
- Popup cua marker hien thi noi dung thong bao su co.
- Thanh ben hien thi trang thai giao thong va thoi gian cap nhat theo giay.
- Cac chuc nang luu tru, gui phan anh, tai khoan, thong bao va quan tri la phan de xuat phat trien tiep.
